Our Story Our Team

Our Journey

Helping Hands is a registered non-governmental organization that works with rural and underserved communities to improve access to education, healthcare, and livelihood opportunities.

Founded in 2015 by Ravi Sharma and Anjali Verma, we began with a small team of 5 volunteers working in a single village. Today, we operate across 3 states with over 1000 dedicated volunteers.

2015 - Foundation

Established with focus on education in rural Maharashtra

2017 - Health Initiatives

Launched first mobile health clinic program

2019 - Women Empowerment

Started vocational training centers for women

Our Vision

To build a just and equitable society where everyone has access to basic rights, opportunities, and well-being regardless of their background or circumstances.

Our Mission

To work at the grassroots level with integrity and passion, uplifting communities through education, healthcare, and empowerment initiatives that create lasting change.

Our Core Values

Compassion

We approach every individual and community with empathy, understanding their unique challenges and needs.

Integrity

We maintain the highest ethical standards in all our operations and are accountable to our stakeholders.

Innovation

We continuously seek creative solutions to complex social problems through innovative programs.

Collaboration

We believe in the power of partnerships with communities, governments, and other organizations.

Equity

We are committed to serving the most vulnerable populations without discrimination.

Sustainability

Our programs are designed to create long-term impact that continues beyond our direct involvement.
